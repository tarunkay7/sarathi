Before you apply

  • Keep your Aadhaar-linked mobile number handy for OTP
  • A medical certificate (Form 1A) is needed if you are 40 or above
  • DL renewal can be done 1 year before or after expiry
  • Only photo/biometric capture needs an in-person RTO visit

      Sarathi Parivahan Sewa (prototype)

      This is a design prototype reimagining India's RTO driving-licence services around problems documented in publicly filed citizen complaints: unclear payment status, silent application delays, and the absence of a persistent citizen account. All names, numbers, fees, and documents shown are mock data. No real government official, system, OTP, or payment gateway is referenced or contacted.
